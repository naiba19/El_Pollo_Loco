How to Play

Help Pepe cross the desert! Collect coins, grab salsa bottles and defeat the giant Endboss at the end of the level to win the game.

← Move Left
→ Move Right
Space Jump
D Throw Bottle
  • Walk over coins and bottles to collect them – the bars at the top fill up.
  • You can only throw a bottle if you have collected one.
  • A bottle defeats a normal chicken with one hit.
  • The Endboss is tougher – hit him 5 times with bottles.
  • Touching an enemy costs health. If the health bar is empty, the game is over.
